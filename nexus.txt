IndicaBunny the smoking bnuuy's official HUD for Darktide! IndicaHUD adds a library of ready-made blocks to [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/warhammer40kdarktide/mods/1263]HUD Studio[/url]: a full main HUD (health, toughness, ability bar, ammo and peril, coherency and a toughness shield), compact teammate panels, and Mourningstar hub portraits.

[heading]Installation Instructions[/heading]
HUD Studio mods don't add anything to your screen by themselves. You pick the elements you want from HUD Studio's library, and each element is called a [b]Block[/b].

[i]The steps below assume you have HUD Studio installed.[/i]

[list=1]
[*]In-game, go to [b]Settings > Mod Settings > HUD Studio[/b].[/*]
[*]Bind a key to the [b]Toggle Editor[/b] setting.[/*]
[*]Close the settings, go to the [b]Meatgrinder or the Mourningstar[/b], and press that key to open the editor.[/*]
[*]At the top of the [b]Blocks[/b] panel, click the [b]Library[/b] button.[/*]
[*]On the left side of the library, click [b]IndicaHUD[/b].[/*]
[/list]
You'll see every block in the mod. Read through what's available, pick the ones you like, and click [b]Add To Canvas[/b] to put them on your screen.

[b]Full sets or single blocks:[/b] The [b]Full Set[/b] entries (Main HUD, Player Panels and Hub Portraits) add a whole section at once. The single blocks let you mix and match. On the Compact Player Panel and Hub Teammate blocks, [b]Read From[/b] picks which player the block shows: for your own set of player panels, add one with Player 2, another with Player 3, and so on until every slot is filled.

See [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/warhammer40kdarktide/mods/1263]HUD Studio's Nexus page[/url] for more on how HUD Studio works.
