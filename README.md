### [IndicaHUD](https://www.nexusmods.com/warhammer40kdarktide/mods/<id>)
<img width="688" height="378" alt="image" src="<preview-image-url>" />

IndicaBunny the smoking bnuuy's official HUD for Darktide. Adds a library of ready-made blocks to HUD Studio: a full main HUD (health, toughness, ability bar, ammo and peril, coherency, toughness shield), compact player panels, and Mourningstar hub portraits. Requires [HUD Studio](https://www.nexusmods.com/warhammer40kdarktide/mods/1263).

**Works with:** The Ability Bar needs [Combat Ability Timers](https://www.nexusmods.com/warhammer40kdarktide/mods/673). Optional: [Player State Indicators](https://www.nexusmods.com/warhammer40kdarktide/mods/635) adds status icons (downed, netted, pounced and more) to the player panels, and [State Your Name](https://www.nexusmods.com/warhammer40kdarktide/mods/1068) formats the names on the hub portraits.

#### Installation Instructions

HUD Studio mods don't add anything to your screen by themselves - you need to open the HUD Studio editor, open the Library, and pick which elements you want. HUD elements in the mod are called **Blocks.**

*The steps below assume that you installed HUD Studio.*

Step by step:
1. In-game, go to **Settings > Mod Settings > HUD Studio**
2. Bind a key to the **Toggle Editor** setting
3. Close the settings, and **in the Meatgrinder / Hub**, press the key you just bound to open the editor
4. In the **Blocks** panel that just opened, at the top, click the **Library** button
5. On the left-hand side of the library, **click on IndicaHUD**

From there, you will see a listing of this mod's HUD elements - have a read at what's available, pick and choose which ones you like, and add them to your screen (Canvas) by clicking on 'Add To Canvas'.

The **Read From** section selects which player's information is being used in the selected block. For a complete set of player panels for example, you would select Player 1 -> Add to Canvas, then Player 2 -> Add to Canvas, etc. until all player slots are filled.

Take a look at [HUD Studio's Nexus page](https://www.nexusmods.com/warhammer40kdarktide/mods/1263) for an explanation of how the mod works.
